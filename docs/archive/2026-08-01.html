malware spear phishing law_firm
  • Cybersecurity researchers have identified a new Go-based loader framework named HollowFrame, which is being used to deliver a Rust-based malware called Matryoshka. The attack begins with a spear-phishing email that contains a link to an encrypted archive, which, when executed, triggers a multi-stage malware installation process.
  • This attack targets law firms, which often handle sensitive information and client data, making them attractive targets for cybercriminals. The deployment of the Matryoshka backdoor could lead to unauthorized access to confidential information, resulting in legal and financial repercussions for affected firms.
