In plain English: A new malware loader is being used to target law firms through spear-phishing.
malware spear phishing law_firm

  • This attack method can lead to unauthorized access to sensitive legal documents and client data, potentially resulting in severe reputational and financial damage for affected firms. The use of sophisticated techniques like encrypted archives and multi-stage payloads indicates a high level of planning and capability from the attackers.
